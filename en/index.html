My Cakes

Moana Cake

Turquoise buttercream frosting with hand-crafted chocolate palm trees, a Maui figure on a popsicle-stick raft, fresh strawberries and blueberries.

Blue Ombré Cake

Elegant white-to-blue colour gradient with hand-piped blue rosettes — simple and striking.

Green Drip Cake

Mint-green buttercream with white chocolate drip, caramel cornflake clusters, white chocolate pieces and cute hand-shaped little figures.

Landscape Cake

Chocolate cake as an island scene: turquoise jelly lagoon, golden biscuit sand, chocolate rocks with green sugar moss — an edible diorama.

Elsa Cake

Turquoise buttercream with white chocolate drip, hand-made sugar glass shards, fondant snowflakes and an Elsa figure as topper.

Chocolate Drip Cake

Light green frosting with generous white chocolate drip, Kinder chocolate pieces, white chocolate balls and chocolate-coated wafer rolls.

Rainbow Cake

Seven colourful sponge layers in all the colours of the rainbow, a donut monster as a fun topper — just as colourful inside as outside.

Butterfly Cake

Light green frosting with colourful fondant butterflies on the sides and multi-coloured piped rosettes on top — a springtime treat.

Home Assistant Cake

White fondant with a hand-shaped Home Assistant logo in blue and fondant tiles showing the real menu items — Areas, Scenes, Integrations, Apps — all around the side.

Toothless Cake

Black fondant with faithfully modelled fins, spines and bright green eyes — Toothless, the Night Fury from "How to Train Your Dragon".

Monster Truck Cake

Multi-level chocolate landscape with tyre tracks, green grass frosting, checkered flags and real monster truck toys — personalised with the name "Matteo".

About me

I bake cakes with a great deal of love and care — by hand, without compromising on ingredients. Every cake is a small work of art that I'm happy to share.
